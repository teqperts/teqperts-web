Instant IMEI & Barcode Lookup

Scan device IMEIs or accessory barcodes directly via your phone's camera. The app automatically checks inventory, matches repeat customers, and verifies Luhn check-digits.

Fast Walk-in Inquiry Mode

Customers asking for quick price quotes don't always give full details. Log quick inquiries with optional contact info, and convert them to repairs with 1 tap when they return.

Advance & Khata Balances

Track deposit advances paid upon repair intake and see remaining balances due on delivery instantly. Avoid lost revenue and disputes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Cellventory require a continuous internet connection?

No. Cellventory is engineered offline-first. All inventory, customer visits, repair history, and khata records operate locally on your phone using SQLite.

Can I backup my store's records?

Yes. You can export all records to standard CSV files with a single tap, which can be saved to Google Drive, emailed, or opened in Microsoft Excel.

What happens if a walk-in customer doesn't want to provide their phone number?

Inquiry Mode allows you to save notes, device details, and price quotations without requiring a phone number or customer name. When they return, you can convert it to a full repair ticket in 1 tap.
